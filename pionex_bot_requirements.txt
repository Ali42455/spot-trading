==================================================
PIONEX SPOT TRADING BOT — SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

1. GENERAL REQUIREMENTS & ARCHITECTURE
- Language: Python
- Exchange: Pionex Spot Trading
- Authentication: API Key and Secret Key securely loaded from a .env file.
- Execution Mode: Automated background scanning and trading.

2. BOT OPERATING RULES & LIMITS
- Scan Frequency: Automatically scans the market every 5 minutes.
- Position Limits: Maximum of 3 open trades simultaneously. No fourth trade can be opened until at least one existing trade is closed.
- Duplicate Prevention: The bot must not open multiple trades on the same coin (no duplicate active coins).
- Trade Amount: Exactly 50 USDT per trade.
- Trade Cycle: Once a trade is closed, the bot must immediately resume searching for a new valid trade opportunity.

3. COIN FILTERING CRITERIA
- Target Pool: Top 20 coins filtered from the market.
- Minimum 24h Volume: >= 8,000,000 USDT.
- Liquidity: High liquidity with tight bid-ask spread (<= 0.10%).
- Trading Pair: USDT Spot pairs only.

4. EMA 20 SCALPING STRATEGY (BUY ONLY)
- Indicator: EMA 20 on a 5-Minute (5M) timeframe.
- Trend Measurement: EMA 20 slope compared to 5 candles ago must be at least +0.10%.
- Trend Candles: At least 4 consecutive completed candles where each candle's Low is above EMA 20.
- Pullback: Price pulls back to touch EMA 20.
- Confirmation Candle: A bullish candle closing above EMA 20.
- Candle Strength: The candle body must be >= 60% of its total high-to-low range.
- Entry Price: Open trade at the beginning of the next candle after confirmation.

5. RISK MANAGEMENT (SL & TP)
- Stop Loss (SL): Placed slightly below the Swing Low of the pullback.
- Take Profit (TP): Calculated as Entry + 2 * (Entry - SL).
- Risk-to-Reward Ratio: 1:2.

6. INVALID CONDITIONS (WHEN NOT TO BUY)
- EMA 20 slope is insufficient (< +0.10%).
- Any of the 4 trend candles touched EMA 20.
- Price did not make a proper pullback.
- Confirmation candle body is less than 60%.
- Confirmation candle closes on the wrong side of EMA 20.
- SL cannot be calculated or risk exceeds allowed limits.
